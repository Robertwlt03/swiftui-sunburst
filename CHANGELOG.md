All notable changes to this project are listed here. The format follows

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added

- `SunburstChart`: any `Identifiable` tree as rings, with hover, click or tap, VoiceOver, branch
  colors and fainter outer rings, on macOS 14 and iOS 17. Taken from storage-manager's explorer.
- `SunburstLayout`: the rings as segments without drawing them, and the piece at a point.
